4. Emails: are you happy for staff to copy and send emails manually, or do you
   want the system to send emails automatically (letters, receipts, reminders)?

5. Extra modules: which of these do you want now, and which can wait?
   Accommodation / Library / Canteen / HR & Payroll / Timetables.

B) DATA EXPORT FROM EDUCIMS

  - payments and account statements per student

If an export is not possible, the attached template (NEW-STUDENTS-TEMPLATE.csv)
can be filled in by hand. The instructions are in 2-HOW-TO-ADD-NEW-STUDENTS.txt.
