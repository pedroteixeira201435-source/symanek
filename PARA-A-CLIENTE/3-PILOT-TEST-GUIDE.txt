SYMANEK SUITE — PILOT TEST GUIDE
Student portal and lecturer tools
==================================================================

WHAT THIS TEST IS FOR
Before every student and lecturer starts using the Suite, we run a short
pilot with one class and one module. The goal is simple: a real lecturer
and a few real students use the system for a few days, exactly as they
would in normal classes, and tell us what worked and what did not.

The Suite:     https://symanek-suite.vercel.app
Works on:      computer, tablet or phone (any modern browser)
Duration:      about 5 working days
Support:       Pedro (WhatsApp) — send a screenshot of any problem


WHO TAKES PART
  1. School administrator (1 person)  — manages students, lecturers, access
  2. Lecturer (1 person)              — teaches the pilot module
  3. Students (3 to 5 volunteers)     — from the same class
  4. Pedro                            — prepares the accounts and gives support

PLEASE DECIDE BEFORE WE START
  [ ] Which class and module we use for the pilot
      (suggested: Auxiliary Nursing, January 2026 intake)
  [ ] Which lecturer teaches it
  [ ] The 3 to 5 student volunteers (full name + email + phone)
  [ ] Who is the school administrator for the Suite
  [ ] Whether the marks entered during the pilot are real marks or practice
      marks. Practice marks will be deleted after the pilot. Published marks
      are locked on the student's record, so we will NOT publish final marks
      during the pilot unless you ask us to.


DAY 1 — ACCESS
------------------------------------------------------------------
Pedro:
  - Enrols the class on its modules and checks the lecturer is allocated.
  - Creates the logins and sends each person a WhatsApp message with the
    link, their username (email) and a temporary password.

Everyone (administrator, lecturer, students):
  - Open the link and sign in with the temporary password.
  - The system asks you to choose your own password. Do it and keep it private.
  - Sign out and sign in again with your new password.


DAYS 2–3 — TEACHING
------------------------------------------------------------------
Lecturer (menu "Lecturer Portal" and "Courseware"):
  1. Check that you see ONLY your own module(s) at the top.
  2. Attendance tab: take the register for a class (tick present/absent,
     choose the date, save). Check each student's attendance %.
  3. Class Board tab: post a notice to your class.
  4. Courseware > Materials: upload a real document (PDF, Word or slides).
  5. Courseware > Assignments: create a short assignment with a due date,
     total marks and, if you like, a brief/worksheet file.

Students (menu "Student Portal" and "Courseware"):
  1. My Studies: check you see your module(s), your lecturer and your
     attendance %.
  2. Announcements: read the lecturer's notice.
  3. Courseware > Materials: open/download the document.
  4. Courseware > Assignments: submit the assignment. Please try it FROM
     YOUR PHONE — attach a file (photo, PDF or Word) and/or type an answer.
  5. Ask Lecturer: send your lecturer a question about the module.


DAYS 4–5 — FEEDBACK AND MARKS
------------------------------------------------------------------
Lecturer:
  6. Courseware > Assignments > Submissions: open each student's file,
     give a mark and written feedback, save.
  7. Student Queries: reply to the students' questions.
  8. Marks tab: enter CA and exam marks and click "Save marks"
     (provisional — students can see them, nothing is locked).
     Do NOT click "Submit to exam board" unless the school decided the
     pilot uses real, final marks.

Students:
  6. Courseware > Assignments: check your mark and the lecturer's feedback.
  7. Ask Lecturer: read the lecturer's reply.
  8. Grades & Transcript: check your provisional marks.

School administrator (any day, menu "Students" and "Programmes"):
  1. Students > Add student: register a (test) new student with programme,
     intake and academic year, status "Enrolled".
  2. Programmes > Cohort Enrolment: enrol that class so the new student gets
     the modules (the preview shows how many; running it twice never
     creates duplicates).
  3. Students > open the student > "Grant portal access": copy the message
     and send it.
  4. Programmes > Lecturers > "+ Add lecturer": register a (test) lecturer,
     then "Grant Suite access" and allocate a module to them.
  5. Edit a detail (e.g. a phone number or email) and check it is saved.
  Tell Pedro which test records you created so he can remove them.

  2. Did you find everything you needed?                   YES / NO

     What were you doing? (screenshot if possible) ________
  4. What was confusing or slow? __________________________

  6. Anything you would like the system to do that it does not do yet?
     ____________________________________________________

After the pilot Pedro fixes what came up, removes any practice data, and
we agree the date to open the Suite to all students and lecturers.


GOOD TO KNOW
  - A lecturer only sees the modules allocated to them, and only the
    students enrolled on those modules.
  - A student only sees their own modules, marks and submissions.
  - Files (materials and submissions) are private: each file can only be
    opened by the lecturer of that module and its enrolled students.
    Maximum 25 MB per file.
  - Forgot your password? The school administrator issues a new temporary
    one: for a student, Students > the student > "Grant portal access";
    for a lecturer, Programmes > Lecturers > "Remove login", then
    "Grant Suite access" again.
  - Nothing is sent by email or SMS yet: logins are sent by WhatsApp and
    students see notices, marks and replies when they open the portal.
