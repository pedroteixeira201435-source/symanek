Symanek Suite · Administrator test

Test the student portal and lecturer tools

Step-by-step guide for Jeremia Fillemon — one test lecturer and one test student
The Suite
symanek-suite.vercel.app
Time needed
About 45–60 minutes
You need
A computer and a phone
Help
Pedro on WhatsApp

What you will do. As administrator you create one test lecturer and one test student, give them access, and then sign in as each of them to check that everything a real lecturer and a real student will do works: attendance, notices, study materials, assignments, marks, feedback and questions.

Why this is safe. The test uses the Certificate in Nutrition and Dietetics, which has no students and no lecturer yet, so no real class, mark or lecturer is touched. The test lecturer and student do not need real email accounts — nothing is sent by email. When you finish, tell Pedro and he removes all test data.

Three windows, three people

The Suite remembers one signed-in person per browser, so keep the three roles apart like this:

1 · AdministratorYou. Your normal browser window on the computer.
2 · Test lecturerA private / incognito window on the computer (Chrome: Ctrl + Shift + N).
3 · Test studentYour phone — this is how most students will use the portal.
A

Your own access

Window 1 · Administrator
1
Open symanek-suite.vercel.app and sign in with the email and temporary password Pedro sent you (jeremiaf@symanekacademy.com).
ExpectA screen “Choose your password”.
2
Type a new password twice and click Save and continue.
ExpectThe Suite opens with the full menu on the left.
B

Set up the test lecturer and student

Window 1 · Administrator
3
Programmes › tab Lecturers › + Add lecturer. Full name Test Lecturer, email test.lecturer@symanekacademy.com, job title Lecturer, department Test › Save.
Expect“Test Lecturer” appears in the staff list, Suite login “None”.
4
On the Test Lecturer row click Grant Suite access › Workspace Lecturer › Grant access. Click Copy message and paste it somewhere (e.g. WhatsApp to yourself) — you need the temporary password.
ExpectA box with the username and a temporary password. Suite login becomes “Active”.
5
Scroll down to Module allocation. Filter the programme CERTIFICATE-NUTRITION-DIETETICS-LEVEL-4. On ANPH402 choose Test Lecturer in the Lecturer column › Save allocation.
Expect“Allocation saved (1)”. Test Lecturer shows 1 module.
6
Students › tab Register › + Add student:
  • Full name Test Student, Student no. TEST0001, email test.student@symanekacademy.com
  • Programme Certificate in Nutrition and Dietetics, Status Enrolled, Year 1
  • Intake January, Academic year 2026 › Save student
ExpectTest Student appears in the register.
7
Programmes › tab Cohort Enrolment. Find Certificate in Nutrition and Dietetics — January 2026 (1 student) › Enrol…. Check the preview › Confirm enrolment.
ExpectPreview: 1 student, 16 modules, 16 new registrations. Registrations column then shows 16.
8
Students › tab Student 360 › select Test Student › Grant portal access › Copy message and keep the temporary password.
ExpectA box with the student's username and temporary password.
