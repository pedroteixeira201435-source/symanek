1 Why we run a pilot

Before every student and lecturer starts using the Suite, one real lecturer and a few real students use it for a few days, exactly as they would in normal classes: taking the register, sharing notes, setting and submitting work, asking questions and receiving marks. They then tell us what worked and what did not, so everything is right before the whole college moves over.

2 Who takes part

School administrator1 person. Manages students, lecturers and access.
Lecturer1 person. Teaches the pilot module.
Students3 to 5 volunteers from the same class.
PedroPrepares the accounts and gives support throughout.

Please decide before we start

  • Which class and module we use (suggested: Auxiliary Nursing, January 2026 intake).
  • Which lecturer teaches it.
  • The 3 to 5 student volunteers (full name, email and phone of each).
  • Who is the school administrator for the Suite.
  • Whether pilot marks are real or practice. Practice marks are deleted afterwards. Final published marks are locked on the student's record, so we will not publish final marks during the pilot unless you ask us to.

3 Day by day

Day 1 — AccessEveryone signs in
Pedro
  1. Enrols the class on its modules and checks the lecturer is allocated.
  2. Creates the logins and sends each person a WhatsApp message with the link, their username (email) and a temporary password.
Administrator, lecturer and students
  1. Open the link and sign in with the temporary password.
  2. Choose your own password when asked, and keep it private.
  3. Sign out and sign in again with your new password.
Days 2–3 — TeachingThe class in the Suite
Lecturer
Menus: Lecturer Portal and Courseware
  1. Check you see only your own module(s) at the top.
  2. Attendance: take the register for a class (present/absent, date, save) and check each student's %.
  3. Class Board: post a notice to your class.
  4. Courseware › Materials: upload a real document (PDF, Word or slides).
  5. Courseware › Assignments: create a short assignment with a due date, total marks and, if you like, a brief.
Students
Menus: Student Portal and Courseware
  1. My Studies: check your module(s), your lecturer and your attendance %.
  2. Announcements: read the lecturer's notice.
  3. Courseware › Materials: open or download the document.
  4. Courseware › Assignments: submit the assignment — attach a file (photo, PDF or Word) and/or type an answer.
  5. Ask Lecturer: send your lecturer a question about the module.
Please submit the assignment from your phone — that is how most students will use it.
Days 4–5 — Feedback and marksClosing the loop
Lecturer
  1. Courseware › Assignments › Submissions: open each student's file, give a mark and written feedback, save.
  2. Student Queries: reply to the students' questions.
  3. Marks: enter CA and exam marks and click Save marks (provisional — students see them, nothing is locked).
Do not click “Submit to exam board” unless the school decided the pilot uses real, final marks.
Students
  1. Courseware › Assignments: check your mark and the lecturer's feedback.
  2. Ask Lecturer: read the lecturer's reply.
  3. Grades & Transcript: check your provisional marks.
Any day — School administratorRunning the school in the Suite
Menus: Students and Programmes
  1. Students › Add student: register a test new student with programme, intake, academic year and status “Enrolled”.
  2. Programmes › Cohort Enrolment: enrol that class so the new student gets the modules. The preview shows how many; running it twice never creates duplicates.
  3. Students › open the student › Grant portal access: copy the message and send it.
  4. Programmes › Lecturers › + Add lecturer: register a test lecturer, then Grant Suite access and allocate a module to them.
  5. Edit a detail (for example a phone number or email) and check it is saved.
Tell Pedro which test records you created, so he can remove them after the pilot.

4 Good to know

Lecturers see only their modulesThey see only the students enrolled on those modules.
Students see only their own recordsTheir own modules, marks, submissions and questions.
Files are privateOnly the module's lecturer and its enrolled students can open them. Maximum 25 MB per file.
Forgot your password?The administrator issues a new temporary one: Students › Grant portal access (students), or Lecturers › Remove login, then Grant Suite access (lecturers).
No emails or SMS yetLogins are sent by WhatsApp. Notices, marks and replies appear when the portal is opened.
Something went wrong?Take a screenshot and send it to Pedro on WhatsApp with what you were doing.
