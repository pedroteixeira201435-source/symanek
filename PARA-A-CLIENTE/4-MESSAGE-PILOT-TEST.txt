Subject: Symanek Suite — ready for a pilot with one class

Good day,

The student portal and the lecturer tools in the Symanek Suite are now ready
to be tested by real users. We have already run the complete process
ourselves with test accounts, so before opening it to the whole college we
would like to run a short pilot with ONE class and ONE module.

During the pilot (about 5 working days):
- the lecturer takes the register, posts a notice, uploads notes, sets a
  short assignment, gives marks with feedback and answers questions;
- 3 to 5 students sign in, see their modules, download the notes, submit
  the assignment (ideally from their phone), ask a question and see their
  marks and feedback;
- the school administrator registers a new student and a new lecturer and
  gives them access, as the school will do in future.

The attached Pilot Test Guide (PDF) explains each
person's steps day by day and ends with a short feedback form.
