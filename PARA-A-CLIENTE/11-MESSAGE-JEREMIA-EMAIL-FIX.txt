Thank you, you found the exact cause.

When a student's email was corrected in the Suite, the change was saved on the
student's record, but their portal login kept the old email underneath. That
is why the new email gave "invalid login credentials" and the old one worked.

It is fixed now:
- From now on, when you correct a student's email, their login moves to the
  new email automatically. Their password stays the same.

New: live online quizzes
Lecturers can now run quizzes online, inside the Suite:
1. Assessments tab -> "+ New test / quiz" -> then "Questions" to write the
   questions (multiple choice, true/false or short answer).
2. "Go live" -> choose the time per student and when it closes.
3. Students open the "Quizzes" tab in their portal and write it against a
   countdown. Answers save automatically.
4. The lecturer watches who has started and submitted on the live monitor.
   The quiz marks itself, and the score goes straight into the gradebook and CA.
