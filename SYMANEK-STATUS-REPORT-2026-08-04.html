1. Executive summary

The public website is live in production and now carries the college's real programmes, fees, admission requirements, official values and banking details. The Symanek Suite (management system) and Student Portal are fully built and the academic engine is complete and matches the college's confirmed rules — but the Suite is still running in demo/offline mode and is not yet connected to the live database. The remaining work to reach full production is well defined and is driven mainly by (a) connecting the Suite to the cloud and loading real accounts/data, and (b) a small set of items still needed from the college.
